BIP-39 Word Reader

Reads the complete BIP-39 English word list aloud, freeing your eyes to verify a physical copy (Entropia tablets or a printout). This is not a seed generator or a seed-phrase reader.

Check the complete inventory, then close this app and remove this device before selecting any seed words. Conduct any lottery-style selection privately, away from cameras, recording-capable devices, and observers. Never enter, search for, or replay your selected seed words here. Inventory completeness does not establish randomness or ceremony security.

Independent project; not affiliated with or endorsed by SeedSigner. For external background, see the SeedSigner Independent Custody Guide.
